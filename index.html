Welcome

A Club Built Around Golf, Hospitality and People

Golf Club has been welcoming golfers, diners and celebrations for years. Whether you're playing a quiet nine holes, bringing a society for the day, or planning a wedding reception, the same team looks after all of it.

  • Eighteen holes of well-maintained parkland golf
  • A clubhouse restaurant and bar open to members and visitors
  • Function spaces for weddings, parties and corporate days
  • Membership for every stage of the game

The Course

Eighteen holes shaped by the natural contours of the land, kept in excellent condition throughout the year. Visitor tee times are welcome every day of the week.

Golf Academy

Pro Shop

Membership for Every Kind of Golfer

Three ways to belong to Golf Club, from full playing rights to purely social.

Social Membership

  • Clubhouse & bar access
  • Invitations to member events

Full Membership

  • Unlimited tee times
  • Priority booking window
  • Clubhouse & dining discounts
  • Annual guest day allowance

Intermediate & Junior

  • Unrestricted course access
  • Reduced green fees for guests
  • Discounted Academy coaching
Weddings, Parties & Corporate Events

Celebrations, Done Properly

From wedding breakfasts to evening receptions, society dinners to corporate away-days, our function suite adapts to whatever you're planning — with in-house catering, dedicated event coordination and the course as your backdrop.

Dining, Open to Everyone

Our restaurant and bar serve breakfast through to dinner, with a terrace looking out over the course. Members and visitors are equally welcome, and we're always happy to take bookings for larger groups.
